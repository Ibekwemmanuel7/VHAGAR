Request body for POST /api/parcel/suitability. No feature pipeline is connected, so only the inputs you supply are used; anything required that you leave out makes the screen refuse to score.

What this is

The same geospatial discipline behind VHAGAR's wildfire tiers, applied to land screening: transparent component scores, provenance on every input, and an explicit completeness statement. It refuses to produce a score when evidence is too thin. It is not a validated land valuation (AVM) or a community-sentiment product.
