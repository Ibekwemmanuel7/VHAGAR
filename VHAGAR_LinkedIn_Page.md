## Overview / About (paste into the About section)

VHAGAR is a nonprofit wildfire intelligence project. We fuse several fire-observing satellites into one honest, near-real-time picture of where fires are burning, how dangerous conditions are, and where fires may spread, so that responders, agencies, and communities can act sooner.

What we do: VHAGAR ingests active-fire detections from both geostationary and polar-orbiting satellites, GOES-18 and GOES-19 from NOAA, and VIIRS (Suomi-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) and MODIS through NASA FIRMS, and fuses them with a parallax-aware algorithm that respects each sensor's real ground footprint. The result is a live operations console covering the United States and Canada, with the underlying design built to extend globally. It shows clustered fire events, fire radiative power, current weather and a spread-risk read, and one-click GeoJSON and KMZ export for GIS and Google Earth.

How we build it:
- Physics core, machine learning at the boundaries.
- Every number states what it measured, and we do not compare measurements that were not made the same way.
- A detection footprint is labeled as a detection extent, never as a burned area, and uncertain detections are flagged rather than hidden.

Why nonprofit: wildfire information should be a public good. VHAGAR is free to use, built on open satellite data, and aimed at operational fire response, research, and community preparedness rather than profit.

## Steps to create the Page

1. On LinkedIn, click the "For Business" grid icon (top right), then
   "Create a LinkedIn Page."
2. Select **Nonprofit**.
3. Enter the Page name (VHAGAR), the public URL, website, industry, size, and
   tagline from above. Tick the verification box and click "Create page."
4. Upload the logo and cover image.
5. Open "Edit page" and paste the Overview/About and Specialties, add a location
   if you want one, then save.
6. Publish the first post above.
7. Optional: apply to the LinkedIn for Nonprofits program
   (linkedin.com/nonprofits) for free member benefits.
